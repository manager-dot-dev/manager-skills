- [Why managers have more bad days](https://newsletter.manager.dev/p/why-managers-have-more-bad-days) — Why Managers Have More Bad Days; guilt, interaction variance, no coding escape; two options for bad days; five root causes of patterns
- [The victim trap of engineering managers](https://newsletter.manager.dev/p/the-victim-engineering-manager) — The Drama Triangle; victim/villain/hero roles; Creator mindset as the exit
- [How To Build The Leadership Habits That Lead To Great Teams](https://newsletter.manager.dev/p/how-to-build-the-leadership-habits-that-lead-to-great-teams) — Weekly Called Shots (now in `feedback` skill); Mon commitment / Fri self-grade accountability habit
