# Hiring — Sources & Further Reading

- [The #1 new engineering managers mistake](https://newsletter.manager.dev/p/the-1-new-engineering-managers-mistake) — Setting Up New Hires for Objective Evaluation; set concrete 30/60/90 day goals in advance, the "would I hire today?" checkpoint, don't ask "am I better with or without them?"
- [You are firing people too late](https://newsletter.manager.dev/p/you-are-firing-people-too-late) — Setting Up New Hires; the 90-day evaluation window, the relief test, don't confuse sunk cost with potential
