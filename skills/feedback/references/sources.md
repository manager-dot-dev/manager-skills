# Feedback — Sources & Further Reading

## manager.dev Articles

- [Being an engineering manager at Amazon](https://newsletter.manager.dev/p/being-an-engineering-manager-at-amazon) — Precision: No Weasel Words; specificity in both critical and positive feedback
- [6 assumptions engineering managers make (and engineers hate)](https://newsletter.manager.dev/p/6-assumptions-engineering-managers) — Public Praise Isn't Universal; ask before assuming how someone wants to be recognized

## External Articles

- [Stop serving the compliment sandwich](https://adamgrant.substack.com/p/stop-serving-the-compliment-sandwich) — Adam Grant — Instead of the Compliment Sandwich; 4-step alternative based on research
- [How to boost remote speed feedback](https://www.bitsandbrushes.news/p/how-to-boost-remote-speed-feedback) — Bits & Brushes — Speed Feedback; building a team-wide feedback habit through structured pairing

## Books

- _Radical Candor_ — Kim Scott — Care Personally, Challenge Directly; the philosophy behind why honest feedback is an act of care
- _The Making of a Manager_ — Julie Zhuo — Opening a Critical Feedback Conversation; discomfort as a signal the conversation is real; silence as data
