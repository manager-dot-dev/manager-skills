# Delegation — Sources & Further Reading

## manager.dev Articles

- [Why Engineering Managers Give Up](https://newsletter.manager.dev/p/why-team-leaders-give-up) — The Team Rep pattern, Epic Ownership, Knowledge Ownership; the root cause of the EM bottleneck
- [What I wish somebody told me during my first year as a manager](https://newsletter.manager.dev/p/a-letter-to-the-first-time-manager) — Team Rep and go-to person trap; first-year delegation mistakes
- [Most micromanagers are blind to being seen as one](https://newsletter.manager.dev/p/most-micromanagers-are-blind-to-being) — Task-Relevant Maturity; Delegation Principles
- [Give your engineers a kingdom](https://newsletter.manager.dev/p/give-your-engineers-a-kingdom) — Kingdom ownership framework; four ownership types with decision authority
- [Who does what and how to support them](https://newsletter.manager.dev/p/who-does-what-and-how-to-support) — Three-Layer Assignment Framework (efficiency / advancement / durability)

## Books

- _High Output Management_ — Andy Grove — Task-Relevant Maturity (TRM); calibrate involvement to the task, not the person's title
- _Turn the Ship Around_ — L. David Marquet — Intent-Based Leadership; "I intend to..." shifts ownership to the team
- _The Dichotomy of Leadership_ — Jocko Willink — Micromanagement vs. Under-management; symptoms of both failure modes
- _Peopleware_ — DeMarco & Lister — Free Electrons; self-directed engineers need orbit, not direction
